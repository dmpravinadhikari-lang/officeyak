# OfficeYak — source material

Screens and sections captured from officeyak.com and resized for a 1080-wide
render. `students`, `attendance` and `reports` are their own product
screenshots, served from their site; the rest are sections of the marketing
page, kept for reference.

The indigo `#15133A` and the bell's pink `#F0407A`, orange `#FF7A1A` and
yellow `#FFC526` in `src/officeyak/brand.ts` are sampled from the page by
pixel count. Outfit and JetBrains Mono are the two faces the site loads.

The bell and the peaks are **not** here: they are drawn in
`src/officeyak/components/`, because the bell has to swing and the range has
to grow. A logo file could do neither.

Nothing in this folder was generated.
